"The Forty Lamps" — a monologue of exactly 100 words, spoken by a dark lord who has lit the forty lamps along the road to his tower every dusk for longer than he can remember. He has raised the dead, fought wars whose names he has forgot, and tonight he hands the hero everything — the crown, the tower, the road, the lamps — with one condition. The tiredness is never announced: it lives in the short sentences, the sagging third step he meant to fix long years ago, and the dusk-to-dawn grind of the lamps. The sting lands in the last line: the dark below is old, and it has not learned his name yet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue (100 words, 480 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
